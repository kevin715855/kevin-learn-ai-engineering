# Context Isolation

Context isolation is about keeping different tasks or areas of knowledge separate when working with Các Mô hình Ngôn ngữ Lớn (LLMs) (LLMs). Think of it like giving each task its own dedicated space. Instead of one big LLM trying to handle everything at once, you use multiple, smaller "agents" that are each focused on a specific job and trained on their own specific data. This prevents unrelated information from interfering with each other, leading to more accurate and reliable results.
