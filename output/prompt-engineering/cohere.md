# Cohere

Cohere offers a suite of closed Các Mô hình Ngôn ngữ Lớn (LLMs) (LLMs) designed for various natural language processing tasks. These models are accessible through an API and are trained and maintained by Cohere, meaning users don't have to worry about the complexities of Huấn luyện (Training) and Tinh chỉnh (Fine-tuning) their own models from scratch. Cohere focuses on providing enterprise-grade NLP solutions emphasizing safety, reliability, and ease of integration.
