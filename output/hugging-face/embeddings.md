# Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings)

Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) are dense, continuous vector representations of data, such as words, sentences, or images, in a lower-dimensional space. They capture the semantic relationships and patterns in the data, where similar items are placed closer together in the vector space. In machine learning, Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) are used to convert complex data into a numerical form that models can process more easily.
