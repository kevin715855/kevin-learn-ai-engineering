# Zero Shot Prompting

Zero-Shot prompting is a Kỹ thuật Tạo câu lệnh (Prompt Engineering) method that relies on the pretraining of a Mô hình Ngôn ngữ Lớn (LLM) (LLM) to infer an appropriate response. In contrast to other Kỹ thuật Tạo câu lệnh (Prompt Engineering) methods, such as Few-Shot prompting, models aren’t provided with Ví dụ of output when prompting with the Zero-Shot technique.
