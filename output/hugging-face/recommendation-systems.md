# Recommendation Systems

In the context of Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings), recommendation systems use vector representations to capture similarities between items, such as products or content. By converting items and user preferences into Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings), these systems can measure how closely related different items are based on vector proximity, allowing them to recommend similar products or content based on a user's past interactions. This approach improves recommendation accuracy and efficiency by enabling meaningful, scalable comparisons of complex data.
