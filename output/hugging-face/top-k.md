# Top-K Sampling

Top-K sampling is a method used by Các Mô hình Ngôn ngữ Lớn (LLMs) (LLMs) during text generation to select the next word. Instead of considering the entire vocabulary, it narrows down the choices to the K most probable words predicted by the model. Low values (1-10) produce conservative, factual outputs. Medium values (20-50) balance creativity and quality. High values (50+) enable diverse, creative outputs. Use low K for technical tasks, high K for creative writing.
