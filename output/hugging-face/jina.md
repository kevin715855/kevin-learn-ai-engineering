# Jina AI

Jina Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) are a family of high-performance, open-source, multilingual, and multimodal Biểu diễn nhúng (Embedding) models developed by Jina AI. They transform text and visual data (like images and charts) into dense numerical vectors for Tìm kiếm ngữ nghĩa, RAG, and AI applications. Các tính năng chính include supporting long contexts (up to 32k Token), multi-task capabilities, and specialized versions for text-to-image and code retrieval.
