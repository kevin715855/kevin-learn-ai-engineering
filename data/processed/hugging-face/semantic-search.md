# Tìm kiếm ngữ nghĩa

Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) are used for Tìm kiếm ngữ nghĩa by converting text, such as queries and documents, into high-dimensional vectors that capture the underlying meaning and context, rather than just exact words. These Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) represent the semantic relationships between words or phrases, allowing the system to understand the query’s intent and retrieve relevant information, even if the exact terms don’t match.
