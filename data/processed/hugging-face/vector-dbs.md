# Các cơ sở dữ liệu Vector

Các cơ sở dữ liệu Vector are specialized systems designed to store, index, and retrieve high-dimensional vectors, often used as Biểu diễn nhúng (Embeddings) that represent data like text, images, or audio. Unlike traditional databases that handle structured data, Các cơ sở dữ liệu Vector excel at managing unstructured data by enabling fast similarity searches, where vectors are compared to find those that are most similar to a query. This makes them essential for tasks like Tìm kiếm ngữ nghĩa, recommendation systems, and content discovery, where understanding relationships between items is crucial. Các cơ sở dữ liệu Vector use indexing techniques such as approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) search to efficiently handle large datasets, ensuring quick and accurate retrieval even at scale.
